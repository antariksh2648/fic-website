About FIC

Risk it for the reward, with the analysis to back it.

The Finance and Investment Club (FIC) at IIM Rohtak is a student-led initiative that fosters a passion for finance and opens career pathways. It runs activities, workshops and competitions that connect academic theory with practical finance.

Mission

Build a deep understanding of financial concepts among IIM Rohtak students through interactive learning; grow readership of Arbitrage and the club's other publications; and run premier events such as the Finance Summit that connect students with finance professionals.

What we do

Four ways to get involved

Read and write

Arbitrage, Corporate Chronicles, Profit Formula, FinSage and the Finance Compendium.

Compete

FAOTY, Mint Your Money, Smart Beta, BID-2-WIN and the club's simulation games.

Learn from practitioners

Guest lectures, workshops and knowledge sessions with industry speakers and alumni.

Practise any time

Quizzes, valuation labs and case simulations in the Finance Arena.
